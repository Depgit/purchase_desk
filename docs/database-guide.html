Rules the whole database follows

  • Nothing is deletedUsers, properties, items and vendors are made inactive, with who, when and why. History always stays complete.
  • History cannot be changedThe activity log and document history are locked by the database itself: no one, not even an admin, can edit or delete them.
  • One table per thingA request and the PO it becomes are one row, so its whole life is in one place. All user × property access is in user_role.
  • Linked and consistentEvery PO, delivery, invoice and advance points to real records, and each change is saved fully or not at all.
  • Sensitive data protectedPasswords and sign-in codes are scrambled; bank account numbers and Zoho credentials are encrypted.
  • Finance approves new mastersNew items, categories, properties, bill types and expense heads stay pending, and unused, until Finance approves them.
